Azeroth Exchange

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Azeroth Exchange is a free, non-commercial fan site that shows World of Warcraft auction house prices. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. This page explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, so we never ask for your name, email, or password.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share information about you with anyone.
  • Your settings are saved only in your own browser.

Settings saved in your browser

To remember your choices between visits, the site saves a few small settings in your browser's local storage. These never leave your device and we can't see them. They are: your selected realm, your faction color theme, your favorite items, your site language, your preferred sort orders, the order of sections on item pages, whether the region median column is shown, and the realms and minimums you choose in the Flip Finder.

You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The site doesn't set any cookies of its own.

Information our server sees

Like every website, the site receives basic technical details when your browser requests a page or file: your IP address, the address requested, the time, and your browser's user-agent string. Visits reach our server through Cloudflare (see below), and our server doesn't keep a log of them. If a request causes an error, such as a missing file, the server's error log records the address requested and the time, but not your IP address. We use that log only to fix problems with the site, and we don't use any of this to identify or profile visitors.

Traffic to the site passes through Cloudflare, which protects and delivers the site. Cloudflare processes these same request details under its own privacy policy.

Other services the site uses

  • Wowhead provides item tooltips and item icons. When you view items, your browser loads those images and tooltips from Wowhead's servers (wowhead.com, zamimg.com), which receive the same basic request details described above. See Wowhead's privacy policy.
  • Blizzard Entertainment supplies the auction house data through its public API. Our server fetches that data; your browser never contacts Blizzard through this site, and we don't send Blizzard anything about you.

Children

The site doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
